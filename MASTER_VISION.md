# BLACK BEACON — Master Vision

## The pitch

**BLACK BEACON** is a cinematic first-person mystery/exploration game. You travel to a
remote North Atlantic island, wracked by storms and heavy fog, to find out why the
automated lighthouse stopped transmitting. The island is abandoned: a derelict fishing
village, wet black rocks, an ancient industrial lighthouse machine.

The lighthouse beam is the signature mechanic. Its light does more than illuminate:
where it rests, it can make visible what ordinary light cannot — hidden structures,
traces of the past, machinery that failed long ago, passages no map records, anomalies
that should not exist. The beam is a searchlight for truth.

## Pillars

1. **Atmosphere is the product.** Loneliness, mystery, cinematic tension. Every system
   exists first to make the island *feel* real: wet surfaces, rolling fog, violent
   storms, volumetric light through rain, believable machinery.
2. **The beam is the identity.** A reusable, data-driven lighthouse-beam system is the
   signature technology. It rotates, it has power states, it fails, and it reveals
   what is hidden. Nothing about it is a one-off scripted event.
3. **Physically believable world.** Realistic PBR materials, wetness, exposure, physics
   of light and fog. The island should feel like somewhere you could actually visit in
   a bad dream.
4. **Craftsmanship and restraint.** Not a jumpscare game. No enemy spam, no inventory
   sprawl, no ten unfinished mechanics. Premium means a small number of things done
   beautifully.
5. **Engineering for humans and agents.** C++ and text/config/data-driven systems that
   are readable, auditable, modular. The project is designed so that future coding
   agents (e.g. Codex) can understand and extend it safely.

## Target feeling

mysterious · lonely · cinematic · atmospheric · realistic · premium ·
physically believable · beautiful

## Reference mood

North Atlantic island · wet rocks · dark ocean · violent storms · heavy fog ·
abandoned fishing village · old industrial lighthouse machinery · realistic interiors ·
wet surfaces · volumetric lighting · a lighthouse beam cutting through fog and rain.

## Setting (as far as 0.1 needs it)

- A small island; one lighthouse, one abandoned village, one landing point.
- The lighthouse was automated decades ago; the last keepers are part of the mystery.
- The player is a visitor/investigator. The story is discovered, not dumped.
- Emotional register: curiosity, awe, solitude — not cheap fear.

## What we are NOT

- Not a jumpscare horror game.
- Not a survival/crafting/inventory game.
- Not an open-world procedural island.
- Not a multiplayer or service game.
- Not a story told in cutscenes — the environment and the beam tell it.

## North star for 0.1

A single coherent evening on the island: arrive in the storm, enter the dark lighthouse,
wake the generator, climb to the lantern room, turn the beam — and let it light up
something that had been standing beside you all along.
